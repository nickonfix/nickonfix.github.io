**AtheroPoint LLC, California, USA** — *May 2024 – Present*
**Principal Investigator:** [Prof. Jasjit S. Suri](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=AKJK7UQAAAAJ&hl=en)

- Built UNet–transformer and Mamba architectures for carotid ultrasound far-wall segmentation.
- Automated carotid intima-media thickness (cIMT) and plaque-burden measurement from the segmented lumen-intima and media-adventitia boundaries.
- Validated the pipeline against expert tracings across **5K+ annotated scans**, using Dice, Jaccard and polyline-distance metrics.
- Deployed **FastAPI/AWS** inference services powering **AtheroEdge 4.0**.

This work is the basis of four journal manuscripts and two U.S. patent applications — see [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
